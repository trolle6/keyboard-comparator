1. Your keyboard

Not sure what socket you have?
  • Pull a switch and look at the PCB. Five holes per key (two small extra ones on the sides) means MX 5-pin. Three holes means MX 3-pin.
  • Budget board shipped with Outemu switches? It probably has Outemu sockets.
  • Thin, flat keyboard? It's low profile: Choc v1 has a rectangular stem with two prongs, Choc v2 and Gateron LP have a cross (+) stem.
  • Switches with no metal pins at all are optical. Adjustable actuation point means magnetic.
